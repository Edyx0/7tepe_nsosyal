# Yankı

## Register

Product UI. Yankı is a Turkish-first social conversation space for keeping up with the public conversation without losing the thread.

## People and purpose

- People who follow news, culture, technology, and local conversations in Turkish.
- The primary job is to scan what matters, join a discussion, and understand the context quickly.
- This prototype must feel immediately legible to people used to dense social timelines while remaining its own product.

## Personality

Direct, warm, current, and considered. The product gives a busy public square a calm editorial rhythm: concise copy, meaningful feedback, and no performative decoration.

## Strategic principles

- Keep the main action—the stream of conversation—within one confident, readable column.
- Preserve familiar social affordances, but use Yankı language and identity rather than any platform trademark or asset.
- Treat the optional context summary as a careful aid, never as authority. Its uncertainty must remain visible.
- Let content density coexist with generous tap targets, keyboard access, and clear focus treatment.
- Device-local memory is appropriate for this demo; it must not imply an account or network connection.

## Anti-references

- A generic analytics dashboard, glassy social concept, or oversized marketing experience.
- Endless rainbow accents, noisy gradients, and engagement bait.
- An imitation of X/Twitter branding, terminology, logos, or visual assets.

## Accessibility

Keyboard-operable navigation and controls, visible focus, at least WCAG AA text contrast, touch-friendly action areas, reduced-motion support, and Turkish-first labels.
